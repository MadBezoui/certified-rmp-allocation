# Data attribution and interpretation

Artur Trindade (2015), **ElectricityLoadDiagrams20112014**, UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI [10.24432/C58C86](https://doi.org/10.24432/C58C86), CC BY 4.0. Original archive: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/321/electricityloaddiagrams20112014.zip (SHA-256 `f6c4d0e0df12ecdb9ea008dd6eef3518adb52c559d04a9bac2e1b81dcfc8d4e1`). Measurements are kW at 15-minute intervals. Documented daylight-saving and pre-activation zeros are retained. `scripts/download_data.py` selects the first 32 source columns with a positive mean in 2012, divides each by its own 2012 mean and keeps 2013 for the experiments. The unweighted sum of the normalized traces is an abstract service resource, not total kW.

The original archive is not redistributed. Capacities, feeders, the supply interruption, ramp limits, service tiers and preference profiles of the demand-trace study are experiment design choices, not dataset annotations; no client is known to be critical. The static benchmark instances are generated by `certrmp/generator.py` from fixed seeds and use no external data.
